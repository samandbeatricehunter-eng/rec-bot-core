# Franchise XP Engine

## Weekly challenge FPP

Highest tier only:
- Bronze 900 FPP
- Silver 1,800
- Gold 3,000

Applies to TEAM and PLAYER challenges. PLAYER challenge also gives PPP to target player.

## Player Performance Dividend

A team's players should help the team earn Franchise XP without summing an entire roster.

Process:
1. use each player's base-game PPP before Dev/rivalry modifiers;
2. rank players on the team;
3. take top five;
4. sum;
5. award 15% as FPP;
6. cap at 2,400 FPP/game.

Example: top-five PPP = 16,500 → 15%=2,475 → cap → 2,400 FPP.

## Media Day commitment FPP

One payout-bearing measurable commitment maximum per team/advance:
- Standard 600 FPP
- Strong 1,200
- High-risk 1,800

Other answers still affect Media/Tweets but do not all become XP events.

## Team results / milestones

- regular-season win: 300 FPP
- legitimate upset bonus: +600
- 3-game win streak threshold: +600
- 5-game: +1,200
- 8-game: +1,800
- playoff win: 1,200
- conference championship win: 2,400
- Super Bowl win: 4,800

Streak thresholds pay once.

## Player recognition dividend

- POTW winner on roster: +300 team FPP
- major season positional award: +600
- MVP/OPOY/DPOY: +1,200

These are team recognition events, not transfers from Player XP.

## Regular game-derived cap

Maximum regular-season FPP attributable to one game: **6,000 FPP = 1.00 XP**.

Includes game-linked challenge, Player Performance Dividend, Media commitment graded by game, and win/result points. Season-ending awards/championship bonus may sit outside this cap.

## Target pace

- poor/low engagement: 6–10 XP
- typical: 10–15
- strong: 14–19
- contender: 18–23
- champion: 22–28

Backtest historical seasons before finalizing 133-XP tree node prices.

## Ownership gap that must be locked

Franchise progression must explicitly belong to either:
A. user-owner identity inside league; or
B. specific team franchise.

The current design leans toward user/franchise progression within league, but implementation should not guess.
