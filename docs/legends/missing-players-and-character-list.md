# Legends Catalog — Missing Players & Character List Research

Research pass against the current 301-player `rec_legend_catalog` (pulled from `docs/legends/shared-catalog-seed.json`, 2026-09-08). Read-only — no catalog or DB changes made.

The existing catalog skews heavily toward Hall-of-Famers and consensus GOAT-tier players at every position. The lists below are calibrated to that same bar for Part 1, and intentionally *below* that bar (on purpose) for Part 2.

---

## Part 1: Notable players missing from the catalog

Organized by the catalog's own position codes. Each entry is a real gap — cross-checked against the full current roster, not a duplicate.

### QB (9)
- **Sid Luckman** — Bears T-formation pioneer, 4x NFL champion, arguably the position's first modern superstar; a startling omission.
- **Fran Tarkenton** — HOF scrambler, all-time passing leader at retirement, 3x Vikings SB starter.
- **Sonny Jurgensen** — HOF pure passer, one of the best arms of the 1960s (Eagles/Redskins).
- **Y.A. Tittle** — HOF, NYG/49ers, iconic bloody-helmet photo, 1963 MVP.
- **Bobby Layne** — HOF, Lions dynasty QB of the 1950s, legendary toughness/swagger.
- **George Blanda** — HOF QB/K, played into his 40s, clutch folk hero.
- **Kurt Warner** — HOF, undrafted grocery-store-to-Super-Bowl story, "Greatest Show on Turf."
- **Aaron Rodgers** — 4x MVP, Packers era-defining talent, future first-ballot HOF.
- **Doug Williams** — First Black QB to start and win a Super Bowl (SB XXII MVP), historically significant.

### HB (6)
- **Marshawn Lynch** — "Beast Mode," 2010 Beast Quake run, Skittles, Seahawks/Bills icon; user-flagged.
- **Curtis Martin** — HOF, 4th all-time rushing yards at retirement; a notable HOF-tier omission.
- **Priest Holmes** — Chiefs era-defining back, then-record 27 rushing TDs in a season (2003).
- **Shaun Alexander** — 2005 NFL MVP, Seahawks single-season TD record at the time.
- **Frank Gore** — 3rd all-time rushing yards, remarkably durable 49ers/multi-team career.
- **John Henry Johnson** — HOF, part of the 49ers' "Million Dollar Backfield."

### WR (8)
- **Tim Brown** — HOF, Raiders career-long star, Heisman winner.
- **Art Monk** — HOF, Redskins possession-receiver great, held career catches record.
- **Lynn Swann** — HOF, Steel Curtain-era Steelers, iconic SB acrobatic catches.
- **John Stallworth** — HOF, Swann's Steelers running mate, 4x SB champ.
- **Paul Warfield** — HOF, Browns/Dolphins deep threat, one of the great pure route runners.
- **Bob Hayes** — HOF, "World's Fastest Man," Cowboys, forced zone defense's invention.
- **Otis Taylor** — Chiefs deep threat, SB IV standout, one of the best of his era.
- **Chad Johnson** — 6x Pro Bowl Bengals WR, legally changed name to "Ochocinco," genuine star-level production.

### TE (3)
- **Ozzie Newsome** — HOF, Browns, one of the position's pioneering pass-catchers.
- **Travis Kelce** — Modern era-defining Chiefs TE, multiple SB titles, all-time TE receiving numbers.
- **George Kittle** — 49ers, elite receiving + blocking two-way TE of the current era.

### C (2)
- **Nick Mangold** — Jets anchor, 7x Pro Bowl, one of the best centers of the 2000s-2010s.
- **Jeff Saturday** — Colts, Peyton Manning's longtime center, 6x Pro Bowl.

### LG / RG (3)
- **Will Shields** — HOF, Chiefs, 12x Pro Bowl guard.
- **Gene Upshaw** — HOF, Raiders, later NFLPA executive director; a big omission given his stature.
- **Zack Martin** — Cowboys, dominant modern guard, All-Pro nearly every season since entering the league.

### LT / RT (3)
- **Walter Jones** — HOF, Seahawks, widely considered among the best pure left tackles ever.
- **Gary Zimmerman** — HOF, Vikings/Broncos, dominant blindside protector.
- **Cal Hubbard** — HOF, one of the sport's earliest great tackles (also an MLB umpire).

### DT (4)
- **Merlin Olsen** — HOF, Rams "Fearsome Foursome," 14x Pro Bowl; a major omission.
- **Curley Culp** — HOF, pioneered the nose tackle position.
- **Cortez Kennedy** — HOF, Seahawks, 1992 Defensive Player of the Year on a bad team.
- **Buck Buchanan** — HOF, Chiefs, one of the first dominant modern interior linemen.

### LE / RE (4)
- **Richard Dent** — HOF, SB XX MVP, anchor of the '85 Bears defense.
- **Carl Eller** — HOF, Vikings "Purple People Eaters."
- **Claude Humphrey** — HOF, Falcons, one of the most feared pass rushers of the 1970s.
- **J.J. Watt** — 3x Defensive Player of the Year, generational Texans/Cardinals pass rusher.

### MLB / LOLB / ROLB (5)
- **Kevin Greene** — HOF, 3rd all-time in sacks.
- **Andre Tippett** — HOF, Patriots, dominant 1980s edge/outside linebacker.
- **Rickey Jackson** — HOF, Saints "Dome Patrol" linebacker corps.
- **Bobby Wagner** — Seahawks, modern all-time great MLB, still active-caliber production into his mid-30s.
- **Zach Thomas** — Dolphins, undersized but dominant tackling machine, 7x Pro Bowl.

### CB (5)
- **Richard Sherman** — Seahawks Legion of Boom, 2013 NFC Championship deflection, All-Pro, Stanford; user-flagged.
- **Darrell Green** — HOF, Redskins, one of the fastest players ever, 20-year career.
- **Aeneas Williams** — HOF, Cardinals/Rams shutdown corner.
- **Emmitt Thomas** — HOF, Chiefs, 1970s ballhawk.
- **Jimmy Johnson** — HOF, 49ers (distinct from the coach of the same name), 1960s-70s shutdown corner.

### FS / SS (4)
- **Ken Houston** — HOF, Redskins/Oilers, redefined the strong safety position.
- **Larry Wilson** — HOF, Cardinals, invented the safety blitz.
- **Emlen Tunnell** — HOF, Giants, first Black player enshrined in Canton, pioneering ball-hawking safety.
- **Earl Thomas** — Seahawks Legion of Boom free safety, elite range and instincts.

### K / P (2)
- **Justin Tucker** — Most accurate kicker in NFL history by career percentage, Ravens.
- **Jason Elam** — Broncos, once tied the NFL record for longest field goal (63 yards).

## Notes on methodology
- Part 1 excludes any name already present in the 301-player catalog (checked against the full extracted roster by position).
- Part 2 lists are deliberately drawn from a lower production tier than Part 1 — the point is character/flavor/variety, not roster strength.
- Draft positions, Heisman years, and career details above reflect widely documented public NFL history; treat as a starting shortlist for review, not a final purchase order — spot-check anything going straight into a migration.
