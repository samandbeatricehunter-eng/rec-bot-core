# Legends Catalog — Missing Players & Character List Research

Research and launch-curation pass against the original 301-player `rec_legend_catalog`
(pulled from `docs/legends/shared-catalog-seed.json`, 2026-09-08). The approved
296-player expansion is now represented in `new-storefront-additions.json` and the
matching database migration.

The existing catalog skews heavily toward Hall-of-Famers and consensus GOAT-tier players at every position. The lists below are calibrated to that same bar for Part 1, and intentionally *below* that bar (on purpose) for Part 2.

---

## Part 1: Notable players missing from the catalog

Organized by the catalog's own position codes. Each entry is a real gap — cross-checked against the full current roster, not a duplicate.

### QB (8)
- **Sid Luckman** — Bears T-formation pioneer, 4x NFL champion, arguably the position's first modern superstar; a startling omission.
- **Fran Tarkenton** — HOF scrambler, all-time passing leader at retirement, 3x Vikings SB starter.
- **Sonny Jurgensen** — HOF pure passer, one of the best arms of the 1960s (Eagles/Redskins).
- **Y.A. Tittle** — HOF, NYG/49ers, iconic bloody-helmet photo, 1963 MVP.
- **Bobby Layne** — HOF, Lions dynasty QB of the 1950s, legendary toughness/swagger.
- **George Blanda** — HOF QB/K, played into his 40s, clutch folk hero.
- **Kurt Warner** — HOF, undrafted grocery-store-to-Super-Bowl story, "Greatest Show on Turf."
- **Doug Williams** — First Black QB to start and win a Super Bowl (SB XXII MVP), historically significant.

### HB (6)
- **Marshawn Lynch** — "Beast Mode," 2010 Beast Quake run, Skittles, Seahawks/Bills icon; user-flagged.
- **Curtis Martin** — HOF, 4th all-time rushing yards at retirement; a notable HOF-tier omission.
- **Priest Holmes** — Chiefs era-defining back, then-record 27 rushing TDs in a season (2003).
- **Shaun Alexander** — 2005 NFL MVP, Seahawks single-season TD record at the time.
- **Frank Gore** — 3rd all-time rushing yards, remarkably durable 49ers/multi-team career.
- **John Henry Johnson** — HOF, part of the 49ers' "Million Dollar Backfield."

### WR (9)

- **Art Monk** — HOF, Redskins possession-receiver great, held career catches record.

- **Paul Warfield** — HOF, Browns/Dolphins deep threat, one of the great pure route runners.
- **Bob Hayes** — HOF, "World's Fastest Man," Cowboys, forced zone defense's invention.

## Part 3: Proposed "Celebs & Could've Beens" storefront category

Curated and added to the storefront catalog on 2026-09-08.
None of the names below appears in the current 301-player shared catalog seed.

This concept works best as two collections under one storefront category:

- **Screen stars** use a fictional football identity, with the performer credited
  beneath it (for example, `Shane Falco — portrayed by Keanu Reeves`). This avoids
  pretending the actor personally had the character's career. Use only one version
  of a repeated character; Paul Crewe is specifically the Burt Reynolds portrayal.
- **Could've beens** use the real athlete's name and derive ratings from documented
  football performance where it exists. Pure athletic projections should be kept
  weaker and more specialized than players with real football resumes.
- **Every game-input identity must have a first and last name.** A one-word stage
  name or character nickname can remain in the UI subtitle, but the catalog/game
  fields use a two-part name (for example, `Calvin Broadus`, not `Snoop`; `Dalip
  Singh`, not `Turley`; and `Bill Goldberg`, not `Battle`).

### 3c. Merge result and ratings boundary

The old "college stars who didn't translate" list is now absorbed rather than
maintained as a competing storefront grouping:

- Charlie Ward plus Eric Crouch, Ty Detmer, Gino Torretta, Danny Wuerffel, Chris
  Weinke, Jason White, Troy Smith, Tommie Frazier, Marcus Dupree, Colt Brennan,
  and Major Harris belong to the 50-player **Celebs & Could've Beens** pool.
- Andre Ware, Rashaan Salaam, Ron Dayne, Robert Griffin III, and Matt Leinart
  belong to **NFL Busts** because each was a prominent NFL draft selection and
  actually entered the league.
- There is no remaining bench: all six former bench names are now included in one
  of the two main category pools.
